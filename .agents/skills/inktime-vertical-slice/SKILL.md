# InkTime Vertical Slice

## Checks

1. Verify roadmap dependencies and current Unity baseline evidence before implementation claims.
2. Identify the smallest persistent domain entities required for this slice; do not expand into a giant lore/content system.
3. Ensure Manual, AI and Hybrid authorship converge on the same structured Script/Publication data model.
4. Define AI/provider requests/results behind InkTime-owned contracts.
5. Require `MockComicAIService` to prove the stage before real local inference is introduced.
6. Keep AI from owning authoritative gameplay decisions such as cash, sales, progression, bankruptcy, canon acceptance or publication approval.
7. Keep persistent canon/relationship changes attributable to their source publication.
8. Keep PDF/export downstream from structured publication data.
9. Search Flux for reusable infrastructure without moving game-specific rules into the submodule.
10. Identify what can be statically tested and what requires real Unity import/compile/Play Mode/export/build evidence.

## Verdict

`READY_FOR_BOUNDED_IMPLEMENTATION`, `MOCK_LOOP_NOT_PROVEN`, `UNITY_BASELINE_REQUIRED`, `DOMAIN_BOUNDARY_VIOLATION`, or `VALIDATION_DEBT_REMAINS`.
